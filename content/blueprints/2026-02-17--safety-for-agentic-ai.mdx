This blueprint is a reminder that “agentic” doesn’t just mean tool use — it also means a larger attack surface. Safety for agentic AI focuses on the controls you need when a model can take actions: preventing prompt injection from becoming tool misuse, reducing accidental data exfiltration, and making it obvious when an agent is operating outside intended policy.

The most reusable part is the lifecycle framing. It’s not enough to add a safety filter at runtime; you want checks during development (red teaming + evals), deployment (configuration, auditability), and operations (monitoring, incident response, versioning/rollbacks). That’s especially relevant when your agent is calling APIs with real privileges.

What to try first: take one existing agent workflow, run it through the blueprint with a handful of intentionally “bad” inputs (prompt injection attempts, requests for secrets/PII), and make sure you can both block the behavior and produce a useful audit trail explaining what was blocked and why.
