This is a LoRA for `Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo` that’s trained with a preference-optimization method (Flow-DPO) rather than a plain supervised objective. The pitch is straightforward: keep the “turbo” workflow (8 steps, guidance ~1.0) but steer the output toward more believable lighting, shadows, and overall contrast.

If you already like Turbo’s speed but find some prompts come out a bit flat, this kind of adapter is a useful pattern: small, composable deltas you can stack or swap without retraining the base model. The model card also includes concrete Diffusers snippets and suggested LoRA scale values.

What to try first: pick 3–5 prompts that reliably produce the “flat” look you want to fix, generate pairs with and without the LoRA (same seed/steps), then sweep the LoRA scale (e.g. 0.6 → 1.0) to find the point where lighting improves without over-baking skin tones or crushing detail.
