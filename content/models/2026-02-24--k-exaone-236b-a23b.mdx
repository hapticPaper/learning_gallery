K-EXAONE-236B-A23B is LG AI Research’s large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) multilingual model: 236B total parameters with 23B active per token at inference. The model card positions it as a strong “generalist” with emphasis on reasoning/agentic tasks and long-context document workflows.

Two details that make it worth a look if you care about deployment (not just benchmarks):

- **256K context support:** the card claims native long-context handling via a hybrid-attention setup plus a sliding window to keep memory under control.
- **Multi-token prediction (MTP):** used for a self-speculative decoding approach that aims to increase throughput without changing your application-level prompting.

If you’re evaluating it, the most revealing tests tend to be long-context tasks: multi-document QA with “needle in a haystack” prompts, or agentic-style planning where the model has to keep constraints straight over a long transcript.
