This is an SDXL-based checkpoint aimed at ACG/anime illustration, with a focus on clean composition, brighter color, and high-detail textures. The model card calls out V6 as a refinement over earlier versions: less "overstyled" output, improved handling of 3D scenes/characters, and an overall brighter look that still holds up at higher resolutions.

The "Hyper" variant is designed to pair with a Hyper-SDXL accelerator LoRA, letting you generate usable results in fewer steps without the usual quality collapse you get from aggressively reducing sampling. If you want a straightforward starting point in ComfyUI or Automatic1111, the author recommends relatively low guidance, around ~10 steps, and Euler a as a sampler; then iterate by adjusting CFG, steps, and your upscaling flow based on the look you're going for.

If you're comparing SDXL checkpoints, a good quick test is to run a handful of consistent prompts (character portrait, full-body pose, and a complex background scene) and see which model keeps anatomy and scene geometry stable while still producing the textures you want.
