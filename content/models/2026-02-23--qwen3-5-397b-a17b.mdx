Qwen3.5-397B-A17B is a very large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) foundation model release from the Qwen team, published in a standard Hugging Face Transformers format (and explicitly called out as compatible with vLLM, SGLang, and similar runtimes). The “A17B” naming highlights that it’s an MoE-style model where only a subset of parameters are active per token.

The model card emphasizes three broad themes:

- **Vision + language unification:** an early-fusion approach meant to make the same base model strong at both text-only and multimodal tasks.
- **Efficiency-minded architecture:** sparse MoE plus additional architectural tricks intended to keep throughput reasonable for its size.
- **Large-scale RL post-training:** reinforcement learning tuned across diverse tasks (reasoning, coding, agents, and visual understanding).

If you’re evaluating it, the fastest “first try” is to benchmark your existing prompt suite (text + a couple real images) and then test tool use / structured outputs, since that’s where big general models often feel most different in practice.
