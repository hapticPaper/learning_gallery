ACE-V1.1 is a YOLOv11 (`ultralytics`) fine-tune intended for **brain tumor detection in MRI scans**. The model card links to an associated BRISC 2025 dataset/paper (arXiv:2506.14318) and includes practical “how to run it” details: it’s trained for 640x640 inputs and the author recommends a confidence threshold of 0.466.

The card also reports evaluation numbers (e.g., mAP50 ~0.9 on a held-out test, plus a claimed **1.00 “background specificity”** focused on reducing false positives in healthy tissue). These are **author-reported values from the upstream model card, not independently verified by this site**, and they may not generalize beyond the referenced dataset/paper.

What to try first: run it on a small labeled MRI set you trust, tune the confidence threshold for your desired false-positive rate, and inspect failure cases manually (missed small lesions vs spurious detections).
