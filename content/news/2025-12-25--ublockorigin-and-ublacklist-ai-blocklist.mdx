This repo is a practical “block the slop” utility: a manually curated list of domains associated with AI-generated imagery, designed to work with both uBlock Origin (ad/content blocking) and uBlacklist (search result filtering). If you’re tired of image searches that are hard to trust, or you want your browsing to default toward human-made sources, this is a simple lever you can pull without installing a new browser or changing your search engine.

The nice part is that it’s just lists: you can subscribe to the project’s URLs, review what’s being blocked, and keep it updated like any other filter list. A good first step is to add the list(s) to uBlock Origin’s filter lists (as a custom list) and then mirror the relevant list into uBlacklist for your preferred search providers. Because it’s community-maintained, treat it as a starting point: you may want to whitelist sites you trust, or contribute new entries when you find repeat offenders.
