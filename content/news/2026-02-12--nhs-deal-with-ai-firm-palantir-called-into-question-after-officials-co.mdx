The Guardian reports on internal concerns raised about the NHS’s relationship with Palantir in the lead-up to a major data-platform rollout. The crux isn’t “AI” in the abstract — it’s whether a national health system can build the data plumbing needed for analytics and automation while maintaining public trust around procurement, oversight, and how patient data is handled.

According to the piece, officials flagged reputational and political risk tied to Palantir’s work with US Immigration and Customs Enforcement, and warned it could slow adoption or create friction across the UK. If you’re tracking real-world “AI in government”, this is a useful reminder that deployment constraints are often social and institutional (consent, governance, and legitimacy), not just technical.
