- AnchorWeave: a technique/demo for image editing or structured visual control.
- Tiny Aya (Cohere Labs): a smaller model line that’s trying to keep the “useful assistant” feel.
- KittenTTS: an open repo for lightweight, hackable TTS.
- AudioX: audio generation/transform work with examples to test.
- Vec2Pix and Code2Worlds: vector/scene workflows that are creeping toward “prompt → world” pipelines.
- MonarchRT: realtime-ish inference ideas.
- Lyria 3 / Seed 2.0: model updates in the music and general media-generation space.
