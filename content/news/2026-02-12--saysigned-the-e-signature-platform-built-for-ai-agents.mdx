SaySigned is an e-signature workflow designed specifically for agentic systems. The core idea is that an agent should be able to generate an agreement (from a template or a structured set of terms), route it to humans for review/signature via email, and then receive a signed artifact back in a machine-consumable way.

What makes this angle interesting is the “agent-to-agent” framing: instead of every agent workflow building a bespoke “send PDF + wait for Docusign” integration, you get a standardized surface (including MCP-style integration) for the whole contract step. A practical first test would be creating a small agreement (NDA or contractor addendum), sending it to yourself, and verifying your agent can detect completion and proceed with the next step (invoice, access provisioning, repo permissions, etc.).
